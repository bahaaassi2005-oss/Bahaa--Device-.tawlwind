Returns & Refunds

Return Policy

We want every customer to enjoy a simple and reliable shopping experience.

Eligible Returns

Products may be eligible for return when the conditions below are met.

  • The product is unused and in good condition.
  • The original packaging is available.
  • The return request is made within 14 days.
  • The product includes its original accessories.
  • Proof of purchase is available.

Non-Returnable Products

Some products may not qualify for a return in the following situations.

  • Damage caused by misuse.
  • Missing accessories or important parts.
  • Physical damage after delivery.
  • Products returned after the allowed period.
  • Products with missing original packaging when required.

Refund Information

Approved refunds are processed after the product has been inspected. The final refund process may depend on the original payment method and the condition of the returned product.

Customers should contact Bahaa Devices before returning a product so the return request can be reviewed first.
