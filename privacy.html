Privacy Information

Bahaa Devices website policy.

Bahaa Devices respects visitor privacy. This website is designed as a front-end e-commerce project, and the information below explains how data entered by users may be handled.

Information We Collect

Visitors may voluntarily provide information through forms on the website. This information may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Contact message
  • Product or order-related information

How Information Is Used

Information entered through the website may be used to respond to messages, provide customer support and support the simulated shopping experience.

Shopping Cart Data

Bahaa Devices uses browser local storage to remember shopping cart information. This may include the selected device, storage capacity, color, quantity and price.

This information is stored locally in the user's browser and is used to keep the shopping cart available while browsing the website.

Sensitive Information

Visitors should not submit passwords, banking information, credit card details or other highly sensitive personal information through this demonstration website.

External Services

The website may use external front-end resources such as Tailwind CSS and Font Awesome. These services may operate according to their own policies.

Data Security

Bahaa Devices aims to keep the website simple and safe. However, this project does not include a production backend or secure online payment processing.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated when website features, pages or functionality change.
